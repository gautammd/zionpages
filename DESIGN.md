# Zion Pages Design System

## Direction

The page should feel exact but not cold. A first-time reader makes one quick,
instinctive choice inside a short thought experiment. That choice reveals one
sharper question and then gets out of the way. A returning reader meets the
connection between the subjects and the writing. Nothing else competes.

The Matrix is a layer beneath the interface: the green-black undertone, a red
point inside the mark, the logo decode, the Construct/Matrix theme control, and
the hidden `redpill` sequence. On the first visit, a skippable neuron-replacement
thought experiment asks for one instinctive answer and responds with one final,
unanswered question. The interaction remembers completion in
`zion_intro_seen_v2` and a stable, versioned answer ID in `zion_answers` for
later personalization. It never scores or labels the reader. The homepage does not
explain or promote the blog; it connects the same subjects before yielding to
the essays. The headline's capsule briefly rolls and reveals its action: red
opens Matrix mode and cobalt blue returns to Construct. The Sanskrit premise
stays in the footer, where Devanagari, transliteration, and translation appear
together. No digital rain, system status, or fan-site copy.

## Typography

- Public Sans carries navigation, headlines, and short explanatory copy.
- Literata is reserved for essay bodies. Use 18px, a 1.78 line height in light
  mode, and 1.84 in dark mode.
- Azeret Mono is reserved for dates, fields, and reading time.
- Keep essay body measure near 65 characters and display headings within 16ch
  when the words allow it.
- Use no more than three weights on a page.

## Color

Backgrounds are soft green neutrals rather than pure white or black. Green is
used for focus, links, and small metadata. Red appears only as the mark's inner
point or the choice that opens Matrix; blue appears only as the choice that
returns. Text never depends on accent color alone.

## Layout

- Use one vertical reading flow.
- Keep content within a 64rem shell and essays within 42rem.
- Use rules only to separate real sections or essay rows.
- Prefer generous space to cards, shadows, panels, and decorative containers.
- Use one primary action per view.
- Present answers as one quiet, single-column sequence. Each phrase is a large
  touch target, never a card, radio control, or survey grid.
- Close the first-visit questions with an icon-only control whose accessible
  name remains explicit.

## Motion

The artificial neuron changes shape once; an answer replaces the question with
a short blur-and-rise transition. The logo may decode on deliberate hover; the
capsule may roll once; and hidden Easter eggs may type a short message.
Reduced-motion mode switches immediately.

## Content

Visible copy must explain something. Avoid words such as “signal,” “system,”
“transmission,” and “decode” unless an article is literally about them. State
uncertainty directly and remove any sentence that only creates atmosphere.
