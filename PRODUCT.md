# Product

## Purpose

Zion Pages is a blog of essays on AI, neuroscience, philosophy, and brain
anatomy.

The articles follow evidence, state uncertainty, and revise the view when facts
demand it.

## Readers

Curious people who want to understand AI, neuroscience, philosophy, and brain
anatomy without being buried in jargon. Prior expertise is not required.

## Editorial Standard

- Say what is known, what is uncertain, and why it matters.
- Prefer plain language to technical performance.
- Use a metaphor only when it makes the idea more accurate.
- Never turn confidence, complexity, or mystery into a substitute for evidence.
- Leave the reader with a clearer question or a usable fact.

## Personality

Precise, calm, honest, and quietly unsettling. The writing should make a reader
question an assumption without making them work to understand the page.

## Design Principles

- Reading is the primary action.
- One clear idea leads each page.
- Typography and space create hierarchy; decoration does not.
- Light and dark themes are equally complete and comfortable.
- Matrix references stay small and functional: theme names, the mark, and hidden
  discoveries. Never visible roleplay.
- No feeds, engagement tricks, dense dashboards, or generic hacker imagery.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep keyboard focus visible, body text at least 16px,
comfortable line lengths, strong contrast, semantic structure, and reduced
motion support.
